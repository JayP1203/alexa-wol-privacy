# Privacy Policy — Send Wake-On-Lan Packet

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

## Overview

Send Wake-On-Lan Packet is a personal Alexa Smart Home skill designed to wake authorized computers using Wake-on-LAN technology.

The skill is intended for private home automation use.

## Information Processed

To provide its functionality, the skill may process:

- Computer names and MAC addresses configured by the skill owner.
- Amazon account-linking identifiers and authorization tokens.
- Technical request information and diagnostic logs necessary to operate and troubleshoot the skill.

The skill does not require users to provide payment information.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to identify authorized devices, process Alexa commands, and request that compatible devices send Wake-on-LAN packets.

Information is not sold or used for advertising.

## Third-Party Services

The skill uses Amazon Alexa and Amazon Web Services for voice-command processing, authentication, and cloud execution. These providers process information according to their applicable privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Control

The skill owner controls the registered device information. Technical logs and authentication information may be retained according to the configured cloud-service settings.

This policy may be updated if the skill's functionality or information-processing practices change.
